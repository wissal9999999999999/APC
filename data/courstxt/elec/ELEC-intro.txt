Introduction and objectives                                                          Program outline



The Intended Learning Objectives of the course


           • Understand the role and importance of basic electronic elements
           • Put into use eletronic devices based on schematic circuits, datasheets and
             documentation
           • Knowledge of digital systems and integrated circuits
           • Knowledge of common inter-chip communication protocols
           • Ability to analyze and synthesize a given prooblem by conducting
             experiments, using standard test equipment and interpret experimental data
           • Have a hand-on ability to program, debug and excecute code on an
             embedded system
           • Understand the chain of instrumentation, acquisition, sensing and actuation
